OUR RETURN POLICY

At SIXPLUS, we believe shopping should be simple. Please review our policy before requesting a return or exchange.

01. Return & Exchange Eligibility

You may request a return or exchange if:

  • You received a damaged or defective product.
  • You received the wrong product, size or colour.
  • You received a product with a manufacturing defect.

Products must be unused, unworn and unwashed, with original tags and packaging intact, subject to applicable consumer protection laws.

02. Return Window

Contact us within 7 days of receiving your order to report an issue. We will review your request and inform you of the next steps.

03. Items Not Eligible for Return

  • Products that have been worn, washed or used.
  • Products damaged through improper use or care.
  • Products missing original tags or packaging.

These conditions do not limit any rights available to customers under applicable law.

04. How to Request a Return or Exchange

Follow these simple steps to submit your request.

STEP 01

Contact Us

Contact our support team with your order number.

STEP 02

Share Details

Explain the issue and share product photos when relevant.

STEP 03

Request Review

Our team will review your request and confirm eligibility.

STEP 04

Get Resolution

We will explain the approved return, exchange or refund process.

Please wait for our instructions before sending a product back.

05. Size Exchanges

Need a different size? Contact us within 7 days of delivery. Exchanges depend on product availability and eligibility.

If the requested size is unavailable, our team will explain the available alternatives.

06. Refunds

If a return is approved, refunds will be processed according to the applicable policy and law. Processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

For Cash on Delivery orders, please contact our support team for the available refund process.

07. Return Shipping

For damaged, defective or incorrect products, we will coordinate an appropriate resolution in accordance with applicable law.

For other eligible requests, any return shipping charges will be communicated before processing.

08. Order Cancellation

Contact us as soon as possible if you wish to cancel an order. Cancellation may not be possible after dispatch. Eligible refunds will be handled according to applicable law.

NEED HELP?

Have a question about your order, return or exchange? Our team is here to help.
