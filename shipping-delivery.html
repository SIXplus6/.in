01. Order Processing

Once your order is placed, we begin processing it. Orders are generally prepared within 1–2 business days, subject to product availability and payment confirmation.

Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays may take additional time to process.

02. Estimated Delivery Time

Delivery times depend on your delivery address, courier service, weather and other logistical conditions.

  • Order processing: approximately 1–2 business days.
  • Transit after dispatch: approximately 4–8 business days.
  • Remote locations may require additional time.

These are preliminary estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates. The actual estimate should be confirmed when your order is placed.

03. Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges will be displayed during checkout before you complete your order.

Any free-shipping offers will be subject to their stated terms and conditions.

04. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, tracking information will be shared when available. You can use the tracking details to check your shipment's progress.

Tracking updates may take some time to appear after the courier receives your package.

05. Delivery Address

Please provide your complete and accurate delivery address, including your PIN code and contact number, when placing your order.

If you need to correct your address, contact customer support as soon as possible. Changes may not be possible once an order is dispatched.

06. Delayed or Undelivered Orders

Unexpected circumstances, courier delays, weather conditions or incorrect address details may affect delivery timelines.

If your order appears delayed or the tracking information has not updated, contact our support team with your order number so we can help.

07. Incorrect or Damaged Deliveries

If you receive an incorrect, damaged or defective product, contact us promptly with your order number and clear photographs where relevant.

We will review the issue and help arrange an appropriate resolution under our Return & Exchange Policy and applicable law.

08. Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) will be available only if the option is enabled for your order and delivery PIN code at checkout.
